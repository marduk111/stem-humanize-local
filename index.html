What to listen for

Attacks (consonants, picks, drum hits)should stay sharp
Sustain and tailscan move slightly

Humanization should be felt more than heard. If you can hear a wobble or a doubled attack, go back and reduce Mix or raise Sense.

Protect the attacks

Every detected attack temporarily pauses the timing movement. Sense decides how easily an attack is detected; Release decides how long the movement stays settled afterwards.

Blend and add expression

Mix decides how much of the humanized layer you hear. Expr adds a very slow level drift so the processed layer does not feel static. Keep the blend low: the moment you can hear two versions at once, transients begin to smear.

Mix0.35
Dry ↔ humanized
Expr0.15
Slow level motion on the wet layer
